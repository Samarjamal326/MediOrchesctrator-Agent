# Clinical Knowledge Base & Guideline Catalog

This document details the multi-domain clinical textbook and guideline hierarchy indexed into the MediOrchestrator ChromaDB vector store.

## Domain Coverage & Benchmark Guidelines

1. **Cardiology**: NIH/NHLBI Adult Treatment Panel Clinical Guidelines (Bookshelf_NBK2003)
2. **Mental Health**: WHO mhGAP Intervention Guide for Mental, Neurological and Substance Use Disorders
3. **Pharmacy**: SAMHSA Clinical Protocols for Substance Abuse and Pharmacotherapy Compendium
4. **Nutrition**: FAO / World Health Organization Food Safety and Micronutrient Risk Assessment Manual
5. **Endocrinology**: NICE NG28: Type 2 Diabetes in Adults Management Guidelines
6. **Pulmonology**: NICE NG115: Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) Management Guidelines
7. **Neurology**: NICE NG128: Stroke and Transient Ischaemic Attack (TIA) Assessment and Care
8. **Oncology**: NICE NG122: Lung Cancer Diagnosis and Management Protocols
9. **Pediatrics**: NICE NG143: Fever in Under 5s - Clinical Assessment and Early Stratification
10. **Emergency**: NICE NG253: Suspected Sepsis Recognition, Risk Stratification and Resuscitation
11. **Gastroenterology**: NICE NG129: Crohn's Disease Assessment and Medical Management
12. **Dermatology**: NICE CG153: Psoriasis Assessment and Clinical Step-Therapy Management
